About Huda

From Iraq to a workbench in Romania

Huda Mahdi works between Iraq and Romania. She trained at the Assamblage Contemporary Jewelry School in Bucharest, and her work has travelled from there to Munich, to Ljubljana, and back home to Bucharest.

Wire is her material. She twists it, bends it and stitches it into lace that never repeats the same way twice, then sets natural stones and pearls into it. Nothing comes out of a mould.

That is why this collection has no stock and no standard sizes — only pieces. Each with its own stones, its own drawing, made once.

“I am proud to say that my pieces are one of a kind, and a design shown once is never repeated. All my work is made from the heart.” Huda Mahdi, for Revista Atelierul
